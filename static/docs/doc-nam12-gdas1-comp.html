Meteorological Input Transition for UW GAM-v2: NAM12 vs. GDAS1 Evaluation

Last Updated: 2026-11-18

1. Background: NOAA NAM12 Retirement and Pipeline Transition

On November 3, 2026, the NOAA National Centers for Environmental Prediction (NCEP) officially retired the North American Mesoscale (NAM) forecast system—including the 12 km CONUS (NAM12) product—and transitioned operational regional forecasting to the Rapid Refresh Forecast System (RRFS) (NWS SCN 26-47; NWS SCN 26-048). On the NOAA Air Resources Laboratory (ARL) HYSPLIT public archive (noaa-oar-arl-hysplit-pds; see also NOAA ARL READY News), the final available NAM12 meteorological file is 2026-11-02, and no subsequent NAM12 files are produced.

The pre-trained UW GAM-v2 models (Lee and Jaffe, 2025) were trained on 2019–2024 ozone-season (Apr–Oct) data using four 12-hour backward HYSPLIT trajectory predictors originally derived from NAM12: trajectory direction (TrajDir), transport distance (TrajDist), water vapor mixing ratio (TrajH2O), and mixing depth (TrajDepth). To ensure continuous operational predictions without interruption, the Smokelyze HYSPLIT batch pipeline switches from NAM12 to GDAS1 (1°, global) beginning November 1, 2026 (cutoff 2026-10-31). Consequently, all UW GAM-v2 predictions from November 1, 2026 onward—including the 2027+ ozone seasons—are driven by GDAS1-derived trajectory predictors alongside the unchanged MERRA-2 and TROPOMI inputs.

2. 2025 Ozone Season Cross-Evaluation (NAM12 vs. GDAS1)

Before finalizing the operational transition, we evaluated how replacing NAM12 (12 km) with uncalibrated GDAS1 (1°, ~100 km) affects downstream UW GAM-v2 outputs using an independent out-of-sample ozone season (April 1 – October 31, 2025) across 748 CONUS AQS O3 sites (N = 151,590 valid paired daily predictions out of 151,875 merged records, including 117,030 non-smoke days and 26,911 smoke days). Both runs used the exact same 2019–2024 pre-trained GAM-v2 models, MERRA-2 meteorology, TROPOMI satellite columns, and HMS smoke flags, differing only in the HYSPLIT meteorological input (NAM12 vs. GDAS1).

3. Operational Decision (No Post-Hoc Calibration) and Interpretation Notes

As shown in Table 1 and Figure 1(c), substituting GDAS1 for NAM12 produces a negligible change in model accuracy against ground-truth observed MDA8 O3: overall non-smoke RMSE shifts from 6.90 ppb to 7.02 ppb (+0.12 ppb), and site-level median RMSE shifts from 6.60 ppb to 6.73 ppb (+0.13 ppb). Direct agreement between NAM12- and GDAS1-driven predictions remains high across CONUS (R2 = 0.950 for MDA8O3_pred; R2 = 0.909 for SMO).

Based on this evaluation, Smokelyze adopts uncalibrated standard GDAS1 trajectory inputs starting November 1, 2026, rather than introducing an additional post-hoc trajectory calibration layer. Stacking site-specific trajectory regression adjustments on top of GAM and EDM carries risks of overfitting, variance compression during extreme meteorological conditions, and reduced transparency and reproducibility. Instead, we preserve the standard meteorological inputs and document the exact transition characteristics below so users can interpret post-2026 results accurately.

Key Interpretation Notes for Post-Nov 2026 (GDAS1-Driven) GAM-v2 Predictions:
• National & Regional Prediction Bias: Nationally, GDAS1-driven MDA8O3_pred has a mean bias of +0.92 ppb (edm_MDA8O3_pred: +1.11 ppb) relative to NAM12, with positive bias across most Eastern and Southern U.S. sites (Figure 1d). Locally, coarser 1° resolution produces larger deviations in complex coastal and mountain terrain, including approximately +3 ppb in the Houston area and −3 to −4 ppb in inland Southern California.
• Smoke O3 (SMO) Estimates: Because background predictions are slightly higher on average, GDAS1-driven SMO on smoke days is estimated 1.29 ppb lower on average (edm_SMO: 1.59 ppb lower) than NAM12-driven estimates.
• Smoke-Influenced Day Classification (smoke_p975): Because the p975 thresholds were derived from NAM12-based training residuals, GDAS1-driven runs flag ~20% fewer smoke-influenced days (smoke_p975: 4,849 → 3,875 days, F1 = 0.822; edm_smoke_p975: 3,883 → 3,018 days, F1 = 0.786), which may slightly raise the 4th-highest MDA8 O3 excluding smoke days.
