MapPost: Collaborative Research Workspace

Last Updated: 2026-07-24

MapPost is a core collaborative feature of the Smokelyze platform, designed to enable researchers and users to document, share, and discuss specific geospatial observations in real-time. It moves beyond simple static notes by preserving the complete analytical context of the map.

1. Getting Started: Authentication

To ensure data integrity and community accountability, MapPost is an identity-based feature.

  • Read-Only Access: Guest users can view existing MapPost pins and read discussions but cannot interact.
  • Full Interaction: You must Sign In (via Google) to create new posts, like existing ones, or reply to threads.
  • Author Identity: Your display name or a custom nickname will be attached to every post you create.

2. How to Create a MapPost

MapPosts are created directly on the Interactive Map page. There are three intuitive ways to initiate a MapPost at a specific location:

1
Quick-Add Mode: Click the "MapPost" toggle in the toolbar to enter placement mode, then click anywhere on the map to drop a pin.
2
Context Menu: Right-click on any location on the map and select "Write" button from the context menu.
3
Drawer Shortcut: Open the MapPost drawer on the right and click the +MapPost button.

- The Submission Form

Once a location is selected, a modal will appear where you can define the following:

  • Title & Text: Provide a descriptive title (max 100 chars) and detailed analysis (max 2000 chars).
  • Visibility Selection: Choose who can see your post (Public / Group / Private).
  • Automatic Metadata: The system automatically attaches the current Target Date and Active Dataset (e.g., GAM-v2) to your post.

3. Advanced Features: Map State Preservation

Unlike traditional comments, MapPosts capture the entire analytical environment at the moment of creation.

Captured Data Description
Coordinates Precise Longitude and Latitude of the observation.
View State The exact Zoom level and Map Center for consistent framing.
Active Layers Every visible GeoJSON and Tile layer (e.g., HMS Smoke, AirNow Markers).
Temporal State The specific date being analyzed on the timeline.

Restore View: Users viewing your post can click the "Get map condition" button to instantly revert their map to exactly what you were seeing, allowing for seamless collaborative verification.

4. Privacy Tiers & Collaboration

Flexible privacy settings allow MapPost to be used for both public dissemination and private research.

Tier Description
Public Visible to the entire Smokelyze community. Ideal for reporting major wildfire events or public health alerts.
Group Restricted to designated research team members. Perfect for internal discussions and data validation.
Private Visible only to you. Use this for personal notes and "Bookmarks" of interesting wildfire patterns.

5. Data Persistence

  • Infrastructure: Powered by Google Firebase Cloud Firestore for real-time, low-latency updates.
  • Scalability: Optimized to handle thousands of concurrent geospatial markers across the CONUS.
  • Security: Granular "Security Rules" ensure that only the original author can edit or delete their posts.
