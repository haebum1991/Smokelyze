Wildfire News Tracking

Last Updated: 2026-07-24

6-hourly tracker for new wildfire-related news using national news RSS feeds, providing narrative situational

1. Key Information

  • Purpose: Aggregate current wildfire news for the US and Canada.
  • Source: Google News RSS via tidyRSS with keyword-based queries.
  • Output: GeoJSON files (News) uploaded daily to GCS.

2. Main Workflow

1RSS Feed Sync: Scrapes Google News using multiple wildfire-related keywords (e.g., "wildland fire", "wildfire smoke", "prescribed fire").
2Geocoding: Maps US state and Canadian province names found in headlines to pre-defined center coordinates for map placement.
3Historical Merge: Loads existing daily GeoJSON from GCS and appends new records, avoiding duplicates.
4Archiving: Saves finalized GeoJSON FeatureCollections to GCS, organized by year.

4. Technical Implementation

- Google News RSS Scraping

Queries Google News using keyword-based RSS feeds and parses results with tidyRSS. Summaries are cleaned of HTML tags and truncated to 300 characters.

# Keyword-based RSS search
NEWS_RSS_URL <- paste0("https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=", URLencode(QUERY))
feed <- tidyRSS::tidyfeed(NEWS_RSS_URL) %>%
  filter(as.Date(item_pub_date) >= q_date)

- State-level Geocoding

News headlines are matched against a built-in lookup table of US states and Canadian provinces to assign approximate geographic coordinates for map visualization.
