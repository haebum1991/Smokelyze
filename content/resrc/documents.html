Impacts of smoke on PM2.5 and O3 concentrations (v6) → PDF

  • This technical note provides a comprehensive overview of the statistical methodologies used to quantify wildfire smoke impacts on surface PM2.5 and O3 concentrations. It details the integration of satellite-derived HMS products with ground-level data, utilizing Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) to establish robust thresholds for smoke influence in non-normal distributions. The document further explains the application of Generalized Additive Models (GAMs) to isolate the wildfire contribution to ambient ozone levels by predicting concentrations in the absence of smoke based on meteorological drivers. A critical highlight is the implementation of Empirical Distribution Matching (EDM) to correct inherent model biases, which significantly enhances the reliability of the analysis and improves the identification of smoke-driven exceedance events by approximately 18% compared to standard models.
